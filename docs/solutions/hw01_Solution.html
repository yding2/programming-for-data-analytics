Question 2. Online Store Sales (20 points)

An online store recorded the number of units sold and the price of five products.

product <- c("Keyboard", "Mouse", "Webcam", "Headphones", "USB Drive")
units_sold <- c(12, 25, 8, 15, 30)
unit_price <- c(45, 20, 65, 35, 12)

a) Product Revenue (5 points)

Calculate the revenue generated by each product. Store the results in a vector named revenue and display it.

revenue <- units_sold * unit_price
revenue
[1] 540 500 520 525 360

b) Total and Average Revenue (5 points)

Calculate the total revenue and the average revenue per product.

total_revenue <- sum(revenue)
average_revenue <- mean(revenue)

total_revenue
[1] 2445
average_revenue
[1] 489

c) High-Revenue Products (5 points)

Display the names of the products that generated more than $500 in revenue.

product[revenue > 500]
[1] "Keyboard"   "Webcam"     "Headphones"

d) Popular and Affordable Products (5 points)

Display the names of the products for which more than 15 units were sold and the unit price was less than $25.

product[units_sold > 15 & unit_price < 25]

Question 3. Working with a Data Frame (35 points)

The following data frame contains health information for three individuals. Height is measured in centimeters, and weight is measured in kilograms.

BMI <- data.frame(
  gender = c("Male", "Male", "Female"),
  height = c(152, 171.5, 165),
  weight = c(81, 93, 78),
  Age = c(42, 38, 26)
)

print(BMI)
  gender height weight Age
1   Male  152.0     81  42
2   Male  171.5     93  38
3 Female  165.0     78  26

a) Calculate BMI (15 points)

Calculate the BMI of each individual, add the results as a new column named BMI, and display the updated data frame.

\[ \text{BMI}=\frac{\text{weight (kg)}}{[\text{height (m)}]^2} \]

BMI$BMI <- BMI$weight / (BMI$height / 100)^2
BMI
  gender height weight Age      BMI
1   Male  152.0     81  42 35.05886
2   Male  171.5     93  38 31.61948
3 Female  165.0     78  26 28.65014

b) Filter by BMI (10 points)

Display the gender and Age of individuals whose BMI is greater than 30.

BMI[BMI$BMI > 30, c("gender", "Age")]
  gender Age
1   Male  42
2   Male  38

c) Filter Using Two Conditions (10 points)

Display the gender of individuals whose BMI is greater than 25 and whose Age is less than 30.

BMI[BMI$BMI > 25 & BMI$Age < 30, "gender"]
[1] "Female"
